# Panda Hacks 2025 – Hackathon Details

## Name
Panda Hacks 2025

## Overview
Panda Hacks 2025 challenges participants to build tools that help high-school students learn better, stay motivated, and reach their full potential. Whether leveraging cutting-edge AI, intuitive design, or creative game mechanics, teams are encouraged to deliver impactful, student-centric solutions.

## Tracks
1. **Agentic AI** – Build intelligent tools that can take action, adapt, and assist students as they study.
2. **No / Low Code** – Create practical solutions quickly with minimal coding; ideal for makers, designers, and first-time builders.
3. **Game Design** – Design educational games that make studying more fun and engaging.

## Problem Statement
Design and build a project that meaningfully addresses real needs for high-school students—improving study effectiveness, engagement, or overall learning outcomes—within one of the three tracks above.

## What to Build (Suggestions)
Projects may take any form that solves a real-world problem, simplifies life, or brings joy. Ideas include but are not limited to:
- Web apps for time, health, or finance management
- Mobile apps connecting local communities
- AI-powered tutoring or mental-health chatbots
- Educational games with compelling narratives
- Cyber-security tools for online safety
- ML models for predictions or recommendations
- Environmental awareness or activism tools

Originality, creativity, and usefulness matter more than perfection.

## Required Deliverables (What to Submit)
By the submission deadline, each team must provide:
1. **Project Submission Form** (via Devpost or the event platform)
2. **Demo Video** (2 – 4 min) – Clearly explain the idea, demonstrate functionality, and highlight unique aspects.
3. **GitHub Repository or Code Files** – Share source code or a working prototype.
4. **Project Description** – Outline inspiration, tech stack, and key learnings.
5. **Team Information** – List all teammates.

Optional but encouraged:
- Screenshots or UI mock-ups
- Slide deck or visual summary

## Judging Criteria (50 pts total)
| Criterion | Points | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Impact & Relevance | 10 | Addresses a real need for high-school students and supports better studying or learning outcomes. |
| Creativity & Innovation | 10 | Originality of the idea or a fresh take on existing tools. |
| Execution & Functionality | 10 | Stability, interactivity, and intelligence/playability/polish of the solution. |
| User Experience & Design | 10 | Clarity, appeal, and student-friendliness of the interface. |
| Presentation & Communication | 10 | Quality of demo video and completeness of write-up. |

## Prize Categories
- **Participation Certificate** – Awarded to all participants (2,000 certificates available).
- **Devpost Achievements** – Potential badges such as “First Online Hackathon,” “X Hackathons lvl 2,” “Hackathon Winner lvl 2,” “Generalist,” “Dual Boot,” etc.
